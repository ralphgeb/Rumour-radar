How the likelihood works

Every 30 minutes the app reads headlines from news sites and asks an AI to judge each rumour: who reported it, how many outlets agree, how specific it is, and whether the company has denied it. When a rumour is later confirmed or denied, it moves to the “Confirmed / denied” tab.

These are estimates and can be wrong. Nothing here is financial advice.
