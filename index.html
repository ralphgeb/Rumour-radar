How it works
At 7:30 and 17:00 (London time) the app reads more than 60 tech, finance and world news sites. A quick first pass drops junk and groups the same story from different outlets. Then an AI reads each story and writes a summary, key points and a full write-up in its own words. It flags rumours (including market-moving world news) with odds of being true, lists stocks the story could move, and writes the Today tech briefing.
Write-ups, odds and stock effects are AI-generated and can be wrong. Not financial advice. Always check the original article.
